About

FinchFolio is an independent one-person digital shop, based in France and selling worldwide through Etsy. We design printable PDF logs and trackers for people who take their hobbies seriously.

Every hobby generates things worth tracking: water tests on an aquarium, a book you meant to finish, a starter you feed every day, a bird you spotted on a walk. FinchFolio turns that tracking into a simple printable sheet, built around real reference numbers and routines instead of generic templates.

Our products are digital downloads. You buy the PDF once, print it at home as many times as you like, and fill it in by hand. There is no physical shipping and no subscription.

FinchFolio is a new shop, currently opening its first product lines on Etsy. Our online presence is built around Pinterest, where we share the covers and pages of our logs.

How we work

  • Reference numbers sourced from real guides, not guessed
  • Printable at home, US Letter and A4
  • One-time purchase, no subscription
  • Built one hobby at a time, not a template mill
  • AI-assisted drafting, human-reviewed content
