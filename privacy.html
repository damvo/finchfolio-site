Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 13, 2026

This policy describes how FinchFolio ("we", "us") handles personal data in connection with this showcase website and with our developer application using the Pinterest API. FinchFolio is an independent, one-person digital shop. For any question, write to finchfolio.shop@gmail.com.

1. Data controller

The data controller is the operator of FinchFolio, reachable at finchfolio.shop@gmail.com.

2. Data collected through this site

This showcase site is a static site hosted on GitHub Pages. It has no form, sets no advertising cookie, and does not directly collect any personal data. For technical and security purposes, the host may log connection data (IP address, browser type) under its own policy.

3. Use of the Pinterest API and Pinterest data

FinchFolio operates a developer application connected to the Pinterest API in order to manage and analyze its own presence on that platform. Through this application, we may access the following data, limited to the Pinterest account of the shop owner or of users who have explicitly authorized the connection:

  • public profile information of the authorized Pinterest account;
  • pins, boards and content published by that account;
  • audience and performance statistics for pins (impressions, saves, clicks).

This data is used exclusively to publish, organize and measure the reach of our own editorial content. We do not use Pinterest data for third-party advertising, we do not resell it, and we do not share it with any unauthorized third party.

4. Legal basis and purposes

Processing is based on the consent given by the user authorizing access to their Pinterest account, and on our legitimate interest in promoting our own content. The purposes are content management, audience measurement, and improving our communication.

5. Data retention

Data obtained through the Pinterest API is kept only as long as necessary for the purposes described above. It is deleted when access is revoked, on request, or when it is no longer useful.

6. Data sharing

We do not sell or rent any personal data. Data may pass through technical providers strictly necessary to operate the service (for example Pinterest and our hosting provider), each bound by its own confidentiality commitments.

7. Compliance with the Pinterest platform

Our use of Pinterest data complies with the Pinterest Developer Terms and the Pinterest Privacy Policy. Users can revoke our application's access at any time from their Pinterest account security settings.

8. Your rights

Because the data controller is based in France, you have, under the GDPR, a right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection and portability over your data. To exercise these rights, write to finchfolio.shop@gmail.com. You may also lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority (in France, the CNIL).

9. Changes

This policy may be updated from time to time. The date of the last update appears at the top of this page.
